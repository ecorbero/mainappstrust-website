2. Privacy policy

The data controller is Main Apps Trust s.l. (details above).

What we collect. If you write to us we receive your email address, your name if you sign with it, and whatever you include in the message. Our hosting provider also records technical data (IP address, browser, date and time) in server logs.

Why, and on what basis. We use your message only to answer it, on the basis of your consent when you contact us (art. 6.1.a GDPR). Server logs are processed on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the site secure (art. 6.1.f).

How long. Enquiries are kept for the duration of the conversation and the time needed for any follow-up, then deleted. Hosting logs are kept for a short period set by the provider, currently around 30 days.

Who sees it. Our hosting provider, GitHub Inc. (GitHub Pages, United States), processes the site's traffic and logs on our behalf under standard contractual clauses. We do not sell or share data with anyone else.

Your rights. You can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability or object to processing by writing to contact@mainappstrust.com. You may also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD), www.aepd.es.

Other. We do not take automated decisions or profile users, and this site is not addressed to children under 14.

3. Cookies and local storage

This site does not use cookies. It runs no analytics, advertising or social plugins, and loads no content from third-party servers.

One local preference. If you switch between the light and dark theme, your choice is stored in your browser's local storage (key mat:theme). It never leaves your device, we cannot read it, and it is not used to identify you. It requires no consent because it only remembers a choice you made.

You can delete it at any time by clearing site data in your browser settings.

Following a link to the App Store or Google Play takes you to a third-party service with its own cookie and privacy practices.
